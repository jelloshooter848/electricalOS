# NEC content position

NFPA holds copyright on NFPA 70 (the National Electrical Code) and enforces it.
Court decisions on standards incorporated by reference (ASTM v. Public.Resource.Org,
D.C. Cir. 2023; ASTM v. UpCodes, 3d Cir. 2026) are moving toward fair use for
reproducing the adopted text, but they are preliminary and circuit-specific.

Our position for v1:

1. We ship no NFPA text. Every reference entry is our own wording.
2. We cite section numbers so the user can read the official text in NFPA Free
   Access or their own copy.
3. Numeric tables we ship (ampacities, areas, factors) are facts needed to apply
   the code, presented in our own layout with our own notes.
4. The AI assistant is instructed to explain and cite, not to quote.
5. We revisit a paid NFPA license after beta if verbatim text is the deciding
   feature for buyers.

Not legal advice. Have counsel review before public launch.
